What We Offer

Intelligent. Fast. Reliable.

Our AI-driven platform provides comprehensive health analysis in seconds, from initial assessment to hospital recommendation.

ML Triage Engine

Advanced machine learning model trained on thousands of clinical records to predict triage severity levels instantly.

Hospital Locator

GPS-based nearby hospital finder using Google Places & OpenStreetMap. Finds the closest ERs based on your location.

Pharmacy Finder

Locate nearby pharmacies for your prescribed medications. Integrated with the AI agent for smart recommendations.

Smart OCR Scanner

Upload prescriptions or lab reports — our computer vision model extracts text and feeds it into the clinical analysis.

How It Works

Enter Your Vitals

Input your heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, SpO2, and symptoms through our clean form interface.

AI Analyzes Instantly

Our ML model predicts your triage level while the AI agent searches clinical guidelines and prepares a detailed assessment.

Get Your Report

Receive a comprehensive clinical summary, triage classification, and nearby hospital suggestions — all in under 10 seconds.

Platform Capabilities

Built for Every Scenario

Whether you're a walk-in patient or arriving by ambulance, our system adapts to your situation.

01

Manual Vitals Entry

Complete form-based interface to input all vital signs, symptoms, and medical history manually.

02

Hardware Band Mode

Connect your IoT health band to automatically stream real-time vitals directly to the AI engine.

03

Emergency Routing

Critical triage levels trigger automatic hospital search and emergency email alerts to your contacts.

04

Visit History

Complete audit trail of every visit — vitals, GPS location, triage results, and AI summaries stored securely.

05

Clinical Guidelines

RAG-powered retrieval of medical guidelines from trusted sources, feeding context to the AI agent.

06

Prescription OCR

Computer vision extracts text from uploaded prescriptions and lab reports for deeper clinical analysis.

07

Multi-Modal AI Agent

LangGraph-powered reasoning engine that combines ML predictions, OCR data, and context for decisions.

08

Secure Data

JWT authentication, local SQLite primary storage with Neon PostgreSQL cloud backup for reliability.
